The restrictions are enforced by the argument types of `Rules`:

### 1.1 `QuotSimpleStatement` (s7:lemSimple)

TeX: "Every sub-layer … is a simple graph: it has no parallel edges, PAR sub-layers have no loops, and
HUB sub-layers are bipartite between hubs and junction copies `[w]`, where the hub `h` and the
junction `w` of any edge `h[w]` are distinct vertices of `G`. Consequently `Q_l` is a simple graph
without isolated vertices."

Back-translation:
1. Two layer edges whose `Q`-edges, each taken in the sub-layer of its own rank, coincide are equal.
   This is "no parallel edges" for the multigraph that the `Finset` would otherwise merge silently.
2. An unpaid PAR object has two distinct junctions.
3. A coloured hub item `(h,u)` with junction `w` has `h ∈ D_l`, `w ∈ Pool_l` and `h ≠ w`.
4. Both ends of every edge of `Q_l` have the same kind, colour and rank, and the rank is `≥ 1`.
   - A PAR edge has colour `< 3M` and joins two junction copies.
   - A HUB edge has colour `< 4M` and joins a hub copy and a junction copy.
   - Every hub copy is a copy of a hub in `D_l`, and every junction copy is a copy of a vertex in
     `Pool_l`.
5. No edge of `Q_l` is a loop.
6. `Q_l` has no isolated vertex.

Verdict: **faithful.** (1) is the substantive content of "no parallel edges". I checked it against
`Rules.rank`: `qEdge ι` is `qEdge 0` retagged, so equal `Q`-edges force equal rank and equal rank-0
class, and `rankOrder` is `Nodup`. (4) states the bipartition in full. (2), (5) and (6) are
definitional, and the docstring says so.

### 1.2 MULT and PAR-MULT (s7:lemMULT, s7 l.753–755)

**`MultStatement`.** For `κ < 4M`, `h ∈ D_l`, `w ∈ Pool_l` with `r(w) = r`: `m_κ(h,w) ≤ multAt r w`.

`κ ∈ [4M_l]`, let `h` be a hub and let `w ∈ Pool_{l,r}`. Then `m_κ(h,w) ≤ mult_r(w)`." `mHub`
matches the TeX definition of `m_κ(h,w)` ("the number of coloured hub items `(h,u)` of colour `κ`
with junction `w`"). **Faithful.** The proof uses `Valid.J1` in the aggregated form ("aggregated over
all parts of round `l`", s6.tex l.637, quoted exactly in the field docstring).

**`MultSublayerStatement`.** For `w ∈ Pool_l`: the number of ranks `ι` such that the junction copy
`(hubTag κ ι false, w)` is a vertex of `Q_l` equals `max_{h ∈ D_l} m_κ(h,w)`. The same holds for hub
copies, with the maximum over `Pool_l`. TeX: "the number of sub-layers `B^{H,(ι)}_κ` (`ι ≥ 1`) in
which `[w]` is not isolated equals `max_h m_κ(h,w)`, and the number of those in which `h` is not

`subLayerRanks` filters on `q.1 = t (rank of q)` and so selects exactly the copies of `x` in the
sub-layers of tag family `t`. **Faithful.**

**`ParMultSublayerStatement`.** The same identity for PAR copies, with
`max_{w' ∈ Pool_l \ {w}} m_κ(w,w')`. TeX: "For a PAR colour `κ` and `w ≠ w'` in `Pool_l` … `[w]` is
not isolated in exactly `max_{w'} m_κ(w,w')` of the sub-layers". `mPar` counts unpaid PAR objects of
colour `κ` with junction pair `s(w,w')` (unordered, as the rank classes are). **Faithful.**

**`MultRunStatement`.** For a run with `3 ≤ l ≤ R` whose past `ofPast …` is `Valid`, every valid
rule, every `ξ`, `κ < 4M_l`, `h ∈ run.D G l`, and `w ∈ poolSet G π l r`:

### 1.3 WellDef (s7:lemWellDef (i)–(v))

**Faithful.**

- `3k_h ≤ 4M` makes each `listOf` a genuine 3-set.

**Faithful.**

**(iv) `WellDefE1Statement`.** For all lists `L`:

### 1.4 Lift (s7:lemLift (i)–(iv)); the refutation target

**(i) `LiftRolesStatement`.** Back-translation:

- two layer edges with an end at `u` in the same sub-layer `(kind, κ, ι)` are equal;
- per middle and colour, at most one PAR object;

**(ii) `LiftDecompStatement`** (the refutation target). Back-translation: for every `Dec` with
`IsDecomp (E(Q_l)) Dec`,
- every cycle member lifts to a single closed walk `c'` with `c'.Nodup`, `|c'| ≥ 3`, and all edges in
  `E(G)`;
- every single-edge member lifts to 1 or 2 non-loop single edges of `G`;
- the concatenated edge list of all lifts is duplicate-free;
- there are at most `2|Dec|` lifted objects.

The TeX says: "For every decomposition `Dec` of `E(Q_l)` into cycles and single edges, the lift of each
cycle … is a cycle of `G`, and the lift of each single edge … consists of one or two single edges of
`G`. The lifts of distinct members of `Dec` are pairwise edge-disjoint. Hence … at most `2|Dec|`
pairwise edge-disjoint objects". **Faithful.** The scratch check confirms two things: the lift of a
cycle is always a singleton `[Obj.cycle _]`, and `(Obj.cycle c').WF ↔ c'.Nodup ∧ 3 ≤ |c'|` holds by
`Iff.rfl`. So the refutation target is exactly the `Nodup` clause.

I re-derived the argument for the Lean `liftObj`/`interior`/`layerOf`. A PAR cycle
`[w_0]…[w_{m-1}]` is lifted edge by edge. The pair `(a, b)` contributes `a.2 = w_i`, followed by
`interior`: `p_i (x_i) q_i`, where `p_i` is chosen by `junction (o, false) = some w_i`, which is well
defined since `w_i ≠ w_{i+1}`. A HUB cycle contributes `h_i u_i` and `w_i u'_i` from any start and in
either direction. The vertices are pairwise distinct by six facts:
1. Copies of one sub-layer carry one tag, so `c.Nodup` gives distinct `w_i`, resp. `h_i`
   (Simple (4)).
2. Distinct `Q`-edges have distinct layer edges (`layerOf` plus rank injectivity), all of colour `κ`.
   So their ports are distinct: WellDef (i) (3) for PAR, `Valid.sdr` `InjOn` for HUB.
3. `p_i ≠ q_i`: `Quot.out` of a non-loop `J^par` edge, and `Nodup` pairing lists for cherries.
4. Middles of same-colour cherries are distinct (WellDef (i) (4)).
5. Hubs, fresh centres and ports are disjoint (`Valid.roles`).
6. Ports, middles and hubs are live, junctions are pooled, and `Live ∩ Pool_l = ∅`.

Adjacency in `G` comes from `J_sub` and `ljv_in` (with `cls_anc`). Edge-disjointness across members
comes from injectivity of junctions at a port, junctions being pooled while ports are not, and
`ljv_J`. **No counterexample.**
